Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Short version: we collect your email to tell you when the MVP opens, we don't sell or share it, and you can ask us to delete it whenever you want.

What we collect

  • Your email address — when you join the waitlist on synathic.com.
  • The database you use (optional) — only if you choose to fill in that field. It's not required to join.

This site does not currently use tracking cookies or analytics scripts. If that changes (for example, if we add product analytics after launch), we'll update this policy first.

Why we collect it

  • To send you one email when the MVP opens, and occasional updates directly related to early access.
  • To understand which databases and stacks people actually use, so we prioritize the right integrations first.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't share it with advertisers or marketing platforms.
  • We don't send you anything beyond what's related to Synathic access and updates.

Where it's stored

Waitlist emails are stored in a PostgreSQL database we operate. As the product grows, we may introduce additional infrastructure or service providers (for example, an email delivery service to send confirmation emails). If that happens before you hear from us otherwise, it will be limited to providers strictly necessary to run the waitlist and product — never to third parties for resale or marketing purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we have, correct it, or delete it entirely, at any time, for any reason. Just email us — we'll handle it within a few days, no forms, no runaround.

Email hellosynathic@gmail.com with "Privacy request" in the subject.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters — for example, if we start using a new third-party processor — we'll update the date at the top and, if the change is significant, email the waitlist directly instead of just quietly editing this page.
